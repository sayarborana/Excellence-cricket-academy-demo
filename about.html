WHO WE ARE

Building Skills, Confidence & Character

Excellence Cricket Academy Ajmer is dedicated to helping aspiring cricketers improve their skills, understand the game, and develop a strong sporting mindset.

We believe cricket is not only about batting, bowling, and fielding. It is also about discipline, teamwork, dedication, and respect.

Our aim is to provide players with an environment where they can learn, practise, grow, and work towards their cricketing goals.

WHAT DRIVES US

Our Vision, Mission & Values

The principles that guide our academy and our players.

Our Vision

To inspire and develop talented cricketers who play with confidence, sportsmanship, and a commitment to excellence.

Our Mission

To help players improve through regular practice, skill development, guidance, and a positive learning environment.
